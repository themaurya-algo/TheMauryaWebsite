03 / SAFETY & CLARITY

Know what the technology
does — and doesn't do.

Automated does not mean risk-free.

Crypto derivatives can be highly volatile. Leverage, liquidation, slippage, fees, gaps, connectivity and software failures can cause substantial losses. Profit targets are not guaranteed.

Read full notices when applying ↗
Does signing up start trading?

No. Registration and Master approval grant account access only. Live trading requires separate verification and authorization.

Is the BTC strategy ready?

No. BTC is shown as a future strategy, not a live or assignable offering.

Does the public site show my positions?

No. Market index quotes are public. Your account and positions remain inside your authenticated dashboard.

Are returns guaranteed?

No. There are no guaranteed returns, and losses can include your entire committed capital.
